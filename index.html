Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. Send me your brief and I will reply with a clear quote, so you know the full price before any work starts.

How long will it take?

Most business websites take 2 to 4 weeks. Online stores with many products can take longer. I give you a timeline in the quote.

Do you work with WordPress and Shopify?

Yes. I build WordPress and WooCommerce sites, Shopify stores, and I can move a store from one platform to the other.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and computers before launch.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business details, logo, any text and photos you have, and a few websites you like. You can upload those inspiration samples in the project brief.

Can I edit the website myself after launch?

Yes. I build it so you can update text, images and products yourself, and I show you how.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I stay available after go-live, and monthly care plans cover updates, backups, security and small changes.

How do payments work?

Payment terms are agreed in your quote. Usually a deposit starts the project and the balance is paid when you are happy with the finished website.
